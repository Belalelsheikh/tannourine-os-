# Host the PWA on Vercel, not Netlify

`README-DEPLOY.md` Step 9 was written for Netlify, but `*.netlify.app` is blocked by Egyptian
fixed-line ISPs while `*.vercel.app` resolves. الحاج (mgmt) and سلمي (finance) work from fixed
lines and must reach RoutesBuilder and the receivables screens, so an unreachable host is a
hard failure for two of the seven roles rather than an inconvenience.

Do not "correct" this back to Netlify on the strength of the deploy guide — the guide is the
stale artifact, and rewriting Step 9 is a tracked ticket.
